Why Solve Order? How is Yootle different?

Solve Order proposes the order in which to attend to what produces results. Each layer supports what comes above it. Employees’ experience is a starting point for understanding where those foundations are strong and where they need attention.

Different tools do different listening jobs. Yootle’s focus is people-led diagnosis, with protected answers, aggregate findings, and the same findings for employees and management. Employers commit to non-retribution when they sign up.

The 90 days start when the first round reaches its agreed reporting minimum. Afterward: $1.99 per provisioned person per month.

Minimum requirements & the card step

The reporting minimum is ordinarily 30 completed responses. A smaller design-partner group needs Troy’s explicit approval of one fixed minimum from 11–29, recorded before invitations. Reaching the minimum alone does not guarantee a report; every release safeguard must pass at close.

In the intended Stripe step, an accepted card is saved without a charge or funds held. Acceptance unlocks the admin step; it does not create the admin account. If the card is declined, retry with corrected details or another card before continuing.

Billing counts each person provisioned to receive a diagnostic once per billing month, even across multiple rounds. You may leave during the free period at no cost; afterward, billing is month to month and you may cancel at any time. Final terms are reviewed during assisted onboarding.

Set up for action

Prepare your
first round.

A setup checklist, a fixed window, and clear notice boundaries.

  1. Create the admin account.

    Verify your work email, set up a passkey, and connect to the approved organization.

  2. Walk through the diagnostic.

    Review the questions, employee invitation, reporting groups, safeguards, and how everyone will receive the outcome.

  3. Complete setup before inviting.

    Fix the employee population, reporting minimum, and exact opening and closing dates and times. Each round has a fixed 17-day window.

Three notices maximum. Later notices go to the entire eligible population through a roster-blind organization-wide channel.

What employees do & what admins can see

Employees start from their private invitation and complete the participant setup before answering. Participation is voluntary. Use a personal device and connection, away from work accounts, machines, and networks.

Employees can revise or withdraw before close using the secret provided at submission. Keep that secret: a lost secret means the answer cannot be revised or withdrawn.

Admins can check setup completion and the fixed schedule. Nobody sees live response counts, progress toward the minimum, or a “minimum met” signal. There are no targeted nonrespondent reminders or extra manager nudges. The active round’s dates and minimum stay fixed.

04

Reports

Learn together.
Decide what’s next.

After the window closes, the same outcome reaches both audiences.

If safeguards pass

A shared lay of the land

The admin receives the released report. All eligible employees receive the identical report within 24 hours of release to management, whether or not they participated.

If safeguards do not pass

No report is released

Everyone eligible receives a suppression notice within 24 hours of close and final determination. Individual answers and unsafe aggregates remain unavailable.

Read the findings as a Perception Map: employee observations across the five areas, including claims versus lived experience. They reflect the people who answered; they do not directly measure customers’ experiences.

Use the findings & schedule another round

Use the shared findings to identify what deserves closer investigation and choose where to act. A reporting minimum is only one protection; release also depends on the response and disclosure safeguards agreed before the round.

Schedule the next round with fresh participation credentials and its own fixed dates, population, and safeguards. A new round does not reset or change an active round.
